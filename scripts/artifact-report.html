Same-day quoting without adding headcount

Whitfield Heating & Air · HVAC / home services · 2026-07-16

Executive summary

Quote turnaround is the binding constraint on close rate. Leads arrive warm and cool while waiting on a hand-built estimate. The fix is not more estimators; it is removing the retyping between intake and quote.

The current path

  • Call notes captured by hand, then retyped into the CRM.
  • Estimates assembled per job from prior quotes.
  • Turnaround measured in days; competitors quote same-day.

Recommendation

Template the estimate against job type and hours, and let intake write the quote directly. Keep a human on approval, not on data entry.

Expected effect

  • Quote issued the same day intake completes.
  • Estimator time moves from typing to reviewing.
  • No new headcount required.

Next step

Instrument current turnaround for two weeks to set a baseline before changing anything.
